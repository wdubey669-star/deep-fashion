NEED HELP?

Quick answers.

Everything a first-time online shopper needs before placing a demo order.

How do I buy a product?

Open a product card, choose a color, add it to your bag, verify your DEEP account, then proceed to checkout.

What payment methods are available?

The frontend includes EasyPaisa, JazzCash and Credit/Debit Card as demo payment choices. Real payment processing requires a secure backend and official gateway credentials.

Can I return an item?

For this frontend project, Returns & Exchange is shown as a customer-care section. You can later connect it to your real store policy and backend.

Is account verification required?

Yes. The demo checkout asks the visitor to verify their DEEP account before placing an order.
